Each cell is the average daily **rank IC** between the news score and the next day's return, with its t-statistic in brackets (|t| above about 2 is unlikely to be luck). *Close→close* includes the overnight jump the bot can't trade; *tradable* is open to next open, what a position bought at the open actually earns.
